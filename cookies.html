Our Story

Our story begins in the heart of Nepal, where food has always been more than something we eat. It is a way of bringing people together, preserving memories, and sharing love. From the quiet hills where grains sway in the mountain breeze to the warm kitchens where families gather around simple homemade recipes, Nepal has a beautiful tradition of turning humble ingredients into something meaningful. Inspired by this heritage, our food is created with the belief that every ingredient has a story and every bite can carry a memory. We bring together wholesome ingredients such as millet, oats, buckwheat, jwano, coconut, peanuts, chocolate, and walnuts, ingredients that reflect both the natural richness of Nepal and the evolving tastes of today. Each cookie is thoughtfully made to celebrate the goodness of these ingredients while keeping alive the feeling of something homemade and familiar. The earthy taste of millet reminds us of Nepal's fields and hardworking farmers. The gentle warmth of oats brings the comfort of a simple morning. Buckwheat carries the rustic character of the hills. Jwano connects us to the spices and seeds that have long been part of Nepali kitchens. Coconut, peanuts, chocolate, and walnuts add new flavors to this familiar story. Behind every jar is the hope that food can create a small moment of happiness, a moment when someone takes a bite and feels the warmth of home, even when they are far away from it. We are inspired by the people who grow our ingredients, the hands that carefully prepare them, and the families who have passed down their love for food from one generation to another. We believe that the most beautiful foods do not need to be complicated. They simply need to be made with honest ingredients, thoughtful hands, and a little heart. That is why our cookies are more than just a snack. They are a celebration of Nepal's land, its flavors, its traditions, and its people. They represent the meeting of old and new, the familiar taste of our roots presented in a way that fits into modern life. With every jar, we hope to share a small part of Nepal with you, the warmth of a Nepali kitchen, the richness of its fields, the generosity of its people, and the simple joy of sitting together and sharing something delicious. Because behind every cookie is a story, and behind our story is Nepal, a place where even the simplest ingredients can become something made with love. For more information see our Instagram Page.
